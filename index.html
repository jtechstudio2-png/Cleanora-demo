How we work

A delivery model you can audit.

Every contract follows the same five stages. Each one ends with a document you keep — so performance is measured against what was agreed, not what was remembered.

  1. 01

    Survey

    Site walk, asset register review and risk assessment by an operations manager — not a salesperson.

    Output: Site survey report

  2. 02

    Specify

    Task frequencies, staffing model and KPIs written into a specification you sign off.

    Output: Service specification

  3. 03

    Mobilise

    Recruitment, TUPE, vetting, training and equipment delivered to a dated mobilisation plan.

    Output: Mobilisation plan

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Supervised shifts, digital task sign-off and a 24/7 service desk for reactive requests.

    Output: Daily task records

  5. 05

    Report

    Monthly performance reviews against agreed KPIs, with audit evidence and corrective actions.

    Output: Monthly KPI report
