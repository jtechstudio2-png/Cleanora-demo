Overview

Specialist cleaning covers work that requires certified operatives, specialist equipment or additional HSE controls. It can be scheduled within a cleaning or FM contract or commissioned as a standalone programme.

Scope of service

Typical inclusions. Final scope is confirmed in the service specification after site survey.

Building envelope

  • Façade and curtain-wall cleaning
  • Rope access and BMU cleaning
  • Atrium and high-level cleaning
  • Solar panel cleaning

Hygiene & compliance

  • Kitchen extract and ductwork cleaning
  • Biohazard and trauma decontamination
  • Pre-occupancy builders' cleans
  • Pest-damage remediation

Mobilisation

Every contract follows the same dated mobilisation plan, closed out with a report at Day 90.

  1. 1

    Weeks −6 to −4

    Contract award & due diligence

    • Mobilisation lead and contract manager appointed
    • TUPE / incumbent workforce data requested and reviewed
    • Site surveys and asset verification
  2. 2

    Weeks −4 to −2

    Specification & systems

    • Task schedules and frequencies agreed per area
    • Risk assessments, method statements and COSHH register issued
    • CAFM, helpdesk and reporting configured to the KPI schedule
  3. 3

    Weeks −2 to 0

    People & equipment

    • Vetting, induction and site-specific training completed
    • Equipment, consumables and uniforms delivered and inspected
    • Client stakeholder and escalation matrix confirmed
  4. 4

    Day 1 to Day 90

    Service commencement & stabilisation

    • Daily supervisor walk-rounds and weekly client reviews
    • Baseline audits completed in the first 30 days
    • Mobilisation close-out report issued at Day 90

Additional for this service

  • Specialist teams mobilised per programme, typically within 10 working days

Procurement questions

Can specialist work be added to an existing contract?

Yes. Specialist programmes are scheduled through the same helpdesk and reported in the monthly KPI report.

Included in every contract

  • 24/7 service desk
  • Independent quality audits
  • Monthly KPI report
  • Site-specific risk assessments
  • Vetted, trained personnel
