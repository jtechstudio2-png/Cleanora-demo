Overview

Public-sector contracts require security-vetted staff, compliance with procurement frameworks and reporting that stands up to audit. Social value and local employment commitments are often contract terms.

Operating constraints

Conditions every service in this sector must be planned around.

Security & assurance

  • Government security vetting
  • Clear-desk and confidential waste controls
  • Restricted-area access logs
  • Business continuity planning

Procurement

  • Framework terms and pricing schedules
  • Open-book reporting where required
  • Social value and local employment
  • Annual audit support

Personnel & vetting

Completed for every individual before deployment and recorded in the contract personnel register.

  • Government security vetting at the required level
  • Right-to-work verification
  • Data protection training
