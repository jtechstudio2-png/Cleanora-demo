Overview

Who we serve and how we deliver.

We were established to serve buildings where service failure has consequences: hospitals, airports, government estates, industrial plants and high-occupancy commercial property.

We self-deliver our core services through directly employed, vetted and trained personnel, and manage specialist subcontractors under our own quality, HSE and reporting controls.

Operating principles

Four commitments written into every contract.

  1. 01

    Specified before delivered

    Every contract starts with a written specification. We do not mobilise against an undefined scope.

  2. 02

    Measured independently

    Quality audits are carried out by a function that does not report to site management.

  3. 03

    One accountable manager

    Every contract has a named contract manager who owns performance and the KPI report.

  4. 04

    Direct employment

    Core services are delivered by our own employees, not labour agencies, so training and vetting are under our control.
