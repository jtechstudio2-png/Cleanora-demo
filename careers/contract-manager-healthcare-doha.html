Contract Manager — Healthcare

Single point of accountability for a hospital cleaning and support services contract with 400+ staff.

Apply for this role

Responsibilities

  • Own contract performance against the KPI schedule
  • Lead monthly performance reviews with the client
  • Manage site managers, supervisors and the rapid response team
  • Work with IPC on audits, outbreaks and corrective actions

Requirements

  • 5+ years managing healthcare cleaning or FM contracts
  • Working knowledge of national healthcare cleanliness standards
  • Experience reporting to client executives
  • Recognised management or FM qualification

Vetting & checks

  • Occupational health clearance
