Compliance

Keeping a statutory compliance register that stands up to audit

What to record, who owns it, and how to make certificate status visible before it expires.

What a register is for

A statutory compliance register shows, for every asset that requires mandatory testing, when it was last tested, by whom, the result, and when the next test is due. It is the evidence you will be asked for after an incident.

Minimum fields

  • Asset reference and location
  • Applicable regulation or standard
  • Test date, result and certificate reference
  • Competent person or contractor
  • Next due date with advance alert
  • Remedial actions and close-out date

Ownership

The register should be held in the client's CAFM or document system, maintained by the FM provider, and reviewed monthly. Certificates belong to the client and transfer with the contract.
