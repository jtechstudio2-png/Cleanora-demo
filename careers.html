Employment standards

What every employee can expect.

These commitments are audited as part of our ethical labour controls and reported in our annual Modern Slavery Statement.

  • Ethical recruitment

    No recruitment fees, ever. You keep your own documents and are paid directly into your own account.

  • Training to competency

    Paid induction and task-specific training before you work unsupervised, with annual refreshers.

  • Career progression

    Operatives progress to supervisor, site manager and contract manager through our internal programme.

  • Safety first

    Every employee has stop-work authority and access to a confidential reporting line.
