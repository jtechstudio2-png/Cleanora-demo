Certifications

  • ISO 9001:2015

    Quality Management

    Externally certified quality management system covering service design, delivery and audit.

    Annual surveillance audit; recertification every 3 years

  • ISO 14001:2015

    Environmental Management

    Controls for chemicals, waste, water and energy across all contracts.

    Annual surveillance audit; recertification every 3 years

  • ISO 45001:2018

    Occupational Health & Safety

    Risk-assessed methods, competent supervision and incident management for every task.

    Annual surveillance audit; recertification every 3 years

  • BICSc

    Cleaning Competency Training

    Operatives trained and assessed to recognised cleaning competency standards.

    Training records maintained per operative

Policies

  • HSP-01

    Health & Safety Policy

    The organisation's commitment, responsibilities and arrangements for health and safety.

    Reviewed annually

  • MSS

    Modern Slavery & Ethical Labour

    Controls against forced labour, recruitment fees and exploitation in our workforce and supply chain.

    Statement published annually

  • DPP

    Data Protection & Information Security

    Handling of client data, building access data and personnel records.

    Reviewed annually

Assurance

  • INS

    Insurance Cover

    Employer's, public and products liability, and professional indemnity insurance.

    Renewed annually

    Business Continuity

    Contingency plans for workforce, supply-chain and systems disruption.

    Tested annually; reviewed after every activation
