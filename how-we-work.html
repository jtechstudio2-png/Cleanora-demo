How we work

A delivery model you can audit.

Every contract follows the same five stages, the same governance structure and the same reporting cadence. Each stage produces a document the client keeps, so performance is measured against what was agreed.

Delivery stages
5
Quality tiers
3 + joint
Standard mobilisation

Mobilisation close-out

Delivery model

  1. 01

    Survey

    Site walk, asset register review and risk assessment by an operations manager — not a salesperson.

    OutputSite survey report

  2. 02

    Specify

    Task frequencies, staffing model and KPIs written into a specification you sign off.

    OutputService specification

  3. 03

    Mobilise

    Recruitment, TUPE, vetting, training and equipment delivered to a dated mobilisation plan.

    OutputMobilisation plan

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Supervised shifts, digital task sign-off and a 24/7 service desk for reactive requests.

    OutputDaily task records

  5. 05

    Report

    Monthly performance reviews against agreed KPIs, with audit evidence and corrective actions.

    OutputMonthly KPI report

Mobilisation

New contracts start against a dated plan agreed before award. The plan is closed out with a written report at Day 90.

  1. 1

    Weeks −6 to −4

    Contract award & due diligence

    • Mobilisation lead and contract manager appointed
    • TUPE / incumbent workforce data requested and reviewed
    • Site surveys and asset verification
  2. 2

    Weeks −4 to −2

    Specification & systems

    • Task schedules and frequencies agreed per area
    • Risk assessments, method statements and COSHH register issued
    • CAFM, helpdesk and reporting configured to the KPI schedule
  3. 3

    Weeks −2 to 0

    People & equipment

    • Vetting, induction and site-specific training completed
    • Equipment, consumables and uniforms delivered and inspected
    • Client stakeholder and escalation matrix confirmed
  4. 4

    Day 1 to Day 90

    Service commencement & stabilisation

    • Daily supervisor walk-rounds and weekly client reviews
    • Baseline audits completed in the first 30 days
    • Mobilisation close-out report issued at Day 90

Every contract has a named contract manager accountable for performance, with defined escalation.

Quality assurance

Quality is checked at three levels, the last of which is independent of site management. Joint audits are added where the client requires them.

Quality assurance regime

1 · Supervisor checksCarried out bySite supervisorFrequencyEvery shiftOutputTask sign-off in the mobile app

Joint auditsCarried out byClient and contract managerFrequencyAs agreed (weekly in clinical areas)OutputSigned joint audit record

Health & safety

  • Site-specific risk assessments and method statements before Day 1
  • COSHH register and safety data sheets held on site and in the portal
  • Permit-to-work integration for high-risk activities
  • Stop-work authority for every operative
  • Incident reporting within 24 hours and root-cause investigation
  • Monthly HSE statistics, including LTIFR, in the KPI report

Continuous improvement

Contract data drives a monthly cycle of review and corrective action, and a quarterly look at what should change in the specification itself.

  1. 01

    Measure

    KPI, audit and helpdesk data captured continuously in CAFM.

  2. 02

    Review

    Monthly performance review with the client against agreed targets.

  3. 03

    Act

    Corrective actions logged with owners and dates, tracked to close.

  4. 04

    Improve

    Quarterly proposals on specification, technology and cost.
