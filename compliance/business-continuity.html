Business Continuity

Contingency plans for workforce, supply-chain and systems disruption.

Overview

Each contract has a continuity plan covering staff shortages, supply disruption, pandemic response and loss of systems, aligned with the client's own plan.

Scope
All contracts

Controls

Arrangements

  • Relief staff pool per region
  • Minimum stock holdings of critical consumables
  • Offline helpdesk fallback
  • Annual desktop exercise with clients
